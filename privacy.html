Privacy Policy

How All Yono Games handles browsing data, emails and third-party links.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how All Yono Games handles information when you browse our static pages. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Last updated: August 2026.

2. What we are

Our site is mainly static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run user accounts, wallets or payment forms on this domain.

3. Information we may receive

a) Technical data from hosting / analytics

  • IP address, browser type, device type (standard server or analytics logs)
  • Pages visited and approximate region

b) Information you send us

  • If you email yonogamingzonteligram@gmail.com, we receive your address and message content
  • If you message via Telegram, that platform’s privacy rules apply

c) What we do not collect on-site

  • We do not ask for bank OTP, UPI PIN, Aadhaar or app passwords
  • We do not process deposits

4. Cookies & third parties

Pages may use basic cookies or similar tech if analytics (for example Google Analytics) is enabled. Third-party apps you install after clicking Download have their own privacy policies — read those inside each app.

5. How we use information

  • To reply to support or listing emails
  • To understand which pages are popular (aggregated)
  • To fix broken links and improve the directory

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

We may share data only if required by law, or with infrastructure providers (hosting, email) who process it on our behalf. Referral partners receive traffic when you click Download — they do not receive a data dump from our contact form (we have none).

7. Data retention

Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve the request. Server logs follow the host’s default rotation.

8. Your choices

  • You can browse without creating an account
  • You can contact us to request deletion of an email thread you sent
  • You can block cookies in your browser

9. Children’s privacy

This directory is for 18+ users only. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
